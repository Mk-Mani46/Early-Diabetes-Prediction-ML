Identifying the Symptoms

Early detection is critical. Notice these physiological changes and consult MedPredict.

Polyuria (Frequent Urination)

When blood glucose levels are high, your kidneys work overtime to filter out excess sugar, triggering an increased urge to urinate.

Polydipsia (Excessive Thirst)

Because you are losing fluids through frequent urination, your body attempts to replenish those liquids leading to unquenchable thirst.

Lethargy & Fatigue

Without adequate insulin, your body's cells cannot absorb glucose efficiently. This deprives your muscles of raw energy, leading to chronic exhaustion.

Unexplained Weight Loss

When you lose sugar through frequent urination, you also lose calories. At the same time, diabetes may keep the sugar from your food from reaching your cells.
