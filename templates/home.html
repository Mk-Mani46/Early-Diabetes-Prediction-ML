What is Diabetes?

Diabetes is a chronic (long-lasting) health condition that affects how your body turns food into energy.

Most of the food you eat is broken down into sugar (glucose) and released into your bloodstream. When your blood sugar goes up, it signals your pancreas to release insulin. If you have diabetes, your body either doesn't make enough insulin or can't use the insulin it makes as well as it should.

Common Symptoms

If you experience these, consider taking the prediction test.

Frequent Urination

Needing to urinate more often than usual, especially at night.

Excessive Thirst

Feeling thirsty all the time, regardless of how much you drink.

Extreme Fatigue

Feeling tired and weak even after getting enough sleep.

How to Prevent It?

Small lifestyle changes can make a big difference.

Healthy Diet

Eat more fiber, fruits, and veggies. Cut down on sugar.

Active Life

Aim for 30 minutes of moderate exercise daily.

Weight Control

Losing 5-10% of body weight can significantly reduce risk.

No Smoking

Smoking increases insulin resistance. Quitting helps.
