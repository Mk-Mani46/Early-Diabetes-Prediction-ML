Preventative Measures

Type 2 diabetes is largely preventable through proactive lifestyle conditioning.

Nutritional Optimization

Focus on a high-fiber, low-glycemic index diet. Whole grains, legumes, and lean proteins mitigate extreme blood sugar spikes and train your body to naturally regulate insulin levels over time.

Aerobic & Anaerobic Activity

Both cardiovascular aerobic exercise and resistance training anaerobic exercise combat diabetes. They increase insulin sensitivity, which means your body requires less insulin to shuttle glucose to your cells.

BMI Management

Excess fat, particularly visceral fat stored in the abdominal cavity, directly contributes to insulin resistance. Losing just 5% of this fat can exponentially lower your diabetic risk factors.

Smoking Cessation

Nicotine alters cellular chemistry and directly causes cells to stop responding to insulin over time. Studies indicate smokers are 30% to 40% more likely to develop Type 2 diabetes compared to nonsmokers.
